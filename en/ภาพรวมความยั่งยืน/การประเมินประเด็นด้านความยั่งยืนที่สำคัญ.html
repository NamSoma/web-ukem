The Company has analyzed and evaluated material sustainability issues in order to bring them into sustainable management, taking into account stakeholder expectations as well as potential sustainability impacts and risks that affect corporate value. This is based on the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards, covering all three dimensions: Environment, Social, and Economic & Governance.
Step 1 :
Identification of stakeholder and corporate material issues that actually occur and are expected to occur to the Company, both positively and negatively. This is done by studying information from documents, statistics, complaints, and facts arising from both inside and outside the organization, feedback from stakeholder groups, and opinions of the Company's Board of Directors and management from various meetings.
Step 2 :
Prioritization The Board of Directors assigned the management to jointly consider and review the grouping and prioritization of the Company's sustainable development issues, based on the Global Reporting Initiative Standards (GRI Standards) guidelines. The issues were then plotted on a Materiality Matrix, with the horizontal axis (X-axis) showing the level of impact of the Company's operations on the economy, society, and environment, and the vertical axis (Y-axis) showing the level of influence on stakeholders' assessments and decisions. This was then presented to the management and reported to the Audit Committee and the Board of Directors.
Step 3 :
Validation Executives and the Sustainability Working Group jointly agreed on the material sustainability issues and their prioritization, as well as set guidelines for responding to stakeholders on various issues and assigning relevant departments to take action.
