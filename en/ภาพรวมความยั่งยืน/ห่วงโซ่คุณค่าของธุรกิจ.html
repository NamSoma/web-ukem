Business Value Chain

The Company operates its business by emphasizing the participation of stakeholders and the management of sustainability activities throughout the business value chain.

Core Activities and Description

Production Factor Management / Inbound Logistics

Business development is carried out by the Business Development Department, which studies chemical market information and situations globally in collaboration with the Sales Department to select products that meet customer needs and select partners with the potential to deliver products to the Company both domestically and internationally.

Operations

There are two forms of warehousing preparation: 1) Storing products in the Company's warehouses and 2) Storing products in warehouses rented by the Company. Both forms involve warehouse management steps with standard working processes of the Company, which comply with occupational health and safety laws and laws related to hazardous chemical management. The Company packs and stores chemical products according to sizes specified by customers, and product quality and accuracy are checked before every delivery.

Marketing and Sales

Regularly meeting customers to inquire about their product needs, as well as presenting information on the Company's new products and services, with appropriate standard pricing and service management.

After-Sales Service

The Customer Service Department coordinates with the Company's Sales Department and customers to verify daily sales volumes and order details. It coordinates between the Company's warehouse and customers for product delivery and listens to customer feedback and suggestions regarding the Company's products and services. This feedback is analyzed for continuous service improvement, including conducting customer satisfaction assessments.

Product and Service Distribution / Outbound Logistics

The Company stores and distributes products separated by packaging size, such as 20-liter drums, 200-liter drums, and large transport tanks, in appropriate, safe, and convenient storage areas. When the Warehouse Department is notified to prepare products and delivery details, it prepares the products and plans the delivery to ensure safe and on-time shipment.
