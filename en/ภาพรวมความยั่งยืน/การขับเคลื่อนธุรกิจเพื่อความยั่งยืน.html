Integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles into corporate strategy

Clearly defines the framework, roles, and responsibilities of each management level, aligning sustainability policies, goals, and plans across the organization, reducing redundancy, and increasing operational efficiency.

Incorporates sustainability issues into strategic decision-making, along with risk management in environmental, social, and governance aspects, to prevent impacts and ensure business continuity.

Facilitates systematic monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of sustainability progress, enabling management to identify status, issues, and improvement opportunities in a timely manner.

Enhances operational transparency and reflects the commitment to responsible business practices, resulting in confidence and trust from all stakeholder groups.

Integrates sustainability issues into the management structure to create business value alongside social and environmental care, forming the foundation for stable and sustainable long-term growth.

1. Roles and Responsibilities of the Board and Management

The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the organization's management in accordance with laws, regulations, and corporate governance principles, considering the interests of all stakeholder groups equally.

In addition, the Company clearly separates the roles of the Chairman and the Managing Director, and has appointed an Audit Committee to systematically strengthen internal audit processes and risk management.

2. Sustainability Policy and Framework

Focuses on responding to the expectations of both internal and external stakeholders, strictly complying with environmental regulations and relevant laws to minimize negative impacts and promote the quality of life for communities and society.

3. Integration of Sustainability with Corporate Strategy

Integrates ESG issues into strategic decision-making at both policy and operational levels to responsibly create long-term value for the organization, while adhering to transparency, anti-corruption principles, and fair stakeholder relations.

4. Sustainability Risk and Opportunity Management

Comprehensively identifies and monitors ESG risks, integrating them into the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) system to effectively control, prevent, and respond to situations that may impact the business.

5. Stakeholder Engagement

Establishes comprehensive communication channels and engagement methods for shareholders, customers, partners, employees, and communities, emphasizing listening to feedback to improve operational processes and build trust.

6. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Reporting

Senior management regularly reports progress to the Board to evaluate sustainability performance outcomes and ensure they effectively meet defined goals and objectives.

7. Information Disclosure and Transparency

Prioritizes the complete, accurate, and timely disclosure of important information, including financial, business, and ESG data. The Investor Relations unit ensures equal communication with shareholders and stakeholders to strengthen confidence and sustainable organizational growth.
