Roles and Responsibilities of the Board and Management

The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the organization's management in accordance with laws, regulations, and corporate governance principles, considering the interests of shareholders and all stakeholder groups equally. The Board also ensures that management strictly complies with the Company's policies ethically and transparently.

In addition, the Company clearly separates the roles of the Chairman and the Managing Director, and has appointed an Audit Committee to strengthen internal audit processes and systemic risk management.

Sustainability Policy and Framework

The Company establishes business policies focusing on both internal and external stakeholders, strictly complying with environmental regulations and relevant laws. We aim to minimize the social impact of our business operations and promote the quality of life for people in society.

Integrating Sustainability with Corporate Strategy

The Company prioritizes integrating environmental, social, and governance issues into the core corporate strategy, considering them as part of strategic decision-making at both policy and operational levels. This approach supports responsible business growth for all stakeholders and creates long-term overall value for the organization.

The Company's policies include principles of transparency, anti-corruption, and managing stakeholder relations fairly and transparently.

Sustainability Risk and Opportunity Management

The Company has established a process to identify and monitor multidimensional sustainability risks, including environmental, social, and governance aspects, to effectively manage and control potential risks. This process is integrated into the overall enterprise risk management system to ensure timely monitoring and response.

Stakeholder Engagement

The Company defines communication and engagement methods with various stakeholders, ranging from shareholders, customers, partners, and employees to the surrounding communities and society. We emphasize listening to opinions and feedback to improve operations and strengthen confidence in the decision-making process.

Monitoring, Evaluation, and Reporting

The monitoring and evaluation process is a crucial part of sustainability governance. Senior management is responsible for regularly reporting operational progress and results to the Board to ensure that sustainability operations meet the defined objectives and goals.

Information Disclosure and Transparency

The Company prioritizes fully, accurately, and timely disclosing essential information, including financial, business, and other sustainability data. An investor relations unit coordinates communication equally with shareholders, investors, analysts, and other stakeholders.

The sustainability governance structure of Union Petrochemical Public Company Limited is a vital mechanism driving operations to meet economic, social, and environmental management requirements adaptively within the business context and the expectations of all stakeholder groups, while simultaneously enhancing long-term organizational stability and sustainable growth.
