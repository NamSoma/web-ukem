• The Board of Directors has the authority to elect one director as the Chairman of the Board, and may also elect one or more directors as Vice Chairmen. The Vice Chairman has duties as assigned by the Chairman.
  • The Board of Directors has the authority and duty to manage the Company in accordance with its objectives, Articles of Association, and resolutions of shareholders' meetings.
  • The Board of Directors has the authority to appoint non-executive directors as the Audit Committee, appoint non-audit committee directors as the Executive Committee, appoint the Managing Director and Deputy Managing Director, and define the authority, duties, and remuneration of the Executive Committee.


  • The Board's duties are outlined in the Company's Articles of Association, which include supervising and managing the Company in compliance with the Civil and Commercial Code, any other laws prescribing directors' duties, the Company's objectives, Articles of Association, and shareholders' meeting resolutions.
  • Define the vision, direction, strategy, and main business policies, including considering potential risk issues to ensure that formulated strategies are implemented effectively.
  • Define or approve budgets and various investment projects of the Company.
  • Control, monitor, and oversee the implementation of key strategies and policies, including the Company's objectives, goals, operational plans, and assigned budgets.
  • Ensure effective and efficient accounting systems, financial reporting, and auditing, as well as internal control and internal audit systems.
  • Supervise and manage potential conflicts of interest, including connected transactions.
  • Define comprehensive risk management guidelines and ensure management has effective risk management systems or processes in place.
  • Amend, change, improve, or cancel, revoke, or remove stipulations, appointments, assignments, and/or authorizations.
  • Ensure accurate, clear, transparent, reliable, and high-standard information disclosure.
  • Act as leaders and role models in good practices.
  • Appoint the Company Secretary.
