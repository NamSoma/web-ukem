In 2025, the Company had total sales (EBITDA) of 2,631.48 million baht, a decrease of 17.82% from the previous year due to lower sales volumes across all product groups, especially general chemicals (Solvents), plastic resins, and other industrial groups, as well as a decrease in average selling prices compared to the previous year.

However, the Company was able to efficiently manage the cost of sales and distribution costs, resulting in a net profit of 55.42 million baht, an increase of 663.09% from the previous year. This was due to adjusting the sales plan by focusing on managing sales to highly profitable customer groups, and the average cost of goods decreased more than the average selling price, leading to an increase in gross profit.
