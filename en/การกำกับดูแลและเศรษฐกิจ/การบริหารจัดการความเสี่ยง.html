The Company recognizes the importance of risk management in driving the organization towards business expansion and sustainable growth. The current business competition environment is constantly changing, driven by both external and internal factors that may affect the ability to achieve the main goals and missions of the Company and its subsidiaries.

Therefore, a risk management policy has been established as a guideline for the Board of Directors, executives, and all employees as Risk Owners, and to ensure that the Company is prepared and responsive to changes in business conditions by operating according to international standards (COSO-Enterprise Risk Management) along with good corporate governance principles, with the following policies:

  • Require the Board of Directors, executives, and all employees to be responsible for assessing, monitoring, and supporting a systematic and effective risk management process, as well as communicating risk management to employees at all levels of the Company to ensure mutual understanding and risk awareness.
  • Establish a risk management system that complies with good standards to serve as a practical guideline and integrate risk management into the Company's decision-making, strategy formulation, planning, and operations.
  • Establish measures and guidelines to manage residual risks to an acceptable level by considering measures that will effectively reduce the likelihood and/or impact of potential risks, both at the corporate and departmental levels, to avoid potential damages or losses.
  • Ensure that risks affecting the Company's goals are systematically and continuously assessed and monitored, and the process must be regularly reviewed and improved to adapt to both short-term and long-term business changes.
  • Regularly report risk management to the Board of Directors and management, including changes in risks, opportunities, and/or potential impacts, as well as updated risk management guidelines.

The Risk Management Committee, consisting of 4 members selected and appointed by the Board of Directors, is responsible for overseeing and advising on corporate risk management to ensure risks are within the Risk Appetite. The committee reviews and provides opinions on draft policies and corporate risk management frameworks, assesses risk evaluations, considers guidelines and mitigation measures of the Company, and reviews investment plans to ensure the Company has adequate and appropriate risk management. This enables the Company to achieve its set goals and report to the Board of Directors.

The Risk Management Working Group, selected and appointed by the Executive Committee, consists of the heads of all 7 departments: Purchasing, Sales, Accounting & Finance, Warehouse & Transportation, HR, and Central Administration. It is responsible for analyzing, monitoring, establishing risk management plans, and evaluating risks so that residual risks remain at an acceptable level, and reports to the Risk Management Committee.

Strategic Risk

Risk from Few Suppliers and Changes in Supplier Policies

Nature of Risk:

The Company is a distributor of chemical products for leading domestic and international companies, specifically solvent chemicals. In 2025, there have been changes in both manufacturers (refineries) and supplier policies, such as adding distributors in every country, which may increase the Company's business competitors and result in a loss of market share.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Market Expansion: Allocating proactive budgets to reach new target groups and increase market share.
  • Building Good Customer Relationships: By offering products according to customer needs.
  • Internal Collaboration Integration: Coordinating between sales, services, and logistics to improve product delivery capabilities.
  • Proactive Satisfaction Management: Emphasizing immediate resolution of complaints, along with a satisfaction evaluation system for continuous improvement.

Capital Projects Risk

Nature of Risk:

The Company continuously invests to expand future growth and gain competitive advantages. Occasionally, this exposes the Company to the risk that returns on investment may not meet targets, or project execution may face delays.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Preparing investment plans, which requires risk analysis and return on investment evaluation, presenting the investment plans to the Risk Management Committee for consideration and opinion, and submitting them to the Board of Directors' meeting.
  • Assigning project managers responsible for monitoring, evaluating, resolving issues, and reporting to the Executive Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Board of Directors.
  • Continuously evaluating investment performance to enable timely assessment and problem resolution.
  • Establishing an investment working group to review the investment plans of the Company and its subsidiaries.

Dependency on Major Customers

Nature of Risk:

The Company faces challenges from relying on major customers amid macroeconomic risk factors, including the impacts of war and tariff policies that directly affect the purchasing power of trading partners. Furthermore, more new competitors entering the chemical industry have led to fierce price competition. The Company is therefore at risk if major customers reduce their order volumes or switch to competitors.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Market expansion: Allocating proactive budgets to reach new target groups and increase market share.
  • Building good customer relationships: By offering products according to customer needs.
  • Internal collaboration integration: Coordinating between sales, services, and logistics to improve product delivery capabilities.
  • Proactive satisfaction management: Emphasizing immediate resolution of complaints, along with a satisfaction evaluation system for continuous improvement.

Financial Risk

Interest Rate Fluctuation Risk

Nature of Risk:

Due to continuous business expansion and investment in strategic projects, the Company still faces challenges from stagnant interest rates and volatility in the global financial market, in order to strengthen its financial position in 2025.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Closely monitor news and policies of financial institutions. The finance department coordinates with financial institutions to assess and change the interest rate format at the appropriate time to lock in financial costs and reduce risks during periods of rising interest rates.
  • Manage the balance between cash inflows and outflows, including adjusting credit terms (given and received) to be consistent, and focus on optimal working capital turnover to reduce the need for additional borrowing and mitigate the impact of interest rate adjustments.
  • Enter into short-term loan interest rate contracts. In the case of rising interest rates, consider entering into short-term loan contracts by locking in longer credit terms to maintain the original interest rate for as long as possible.

Exchange Rate Fluctuation Risk

Nature of Risk:

Since the Company's business operations are linked to foreign markets, it inevitably faces risks from exchange rate fluctuations, especially in a highly uncertain global economic environment driven by the monetary policies of major central banks.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Assign personnel to closely monitor news and exchange rate movements on a daily basis to accurately assess currency trends, and coordinate and consult with partner banks to obtain their perspectives and currency trend analyses for decision-making.
  • Seek new financial instruments by studying and analyzing a wider variety of financial products to increase options for more flexible risk management.

Supply Chain Risk

Product Price Volatility Risk (High Costs)

Nature of Risk:

In 2025, the Thai economy is expected to expand, driven mainly by robust domestic consumption and public investment. However, the business sector still faces challenges from external factors such as trade wars, monetary policy directions of major powers, and volatility in global energy prices.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Monitor, analyze, and evaluate situations of factors affecting product price volatility, such as global oil prices, economic conditions, changes in supply and demand, and global climate change, to plan product trading.
  • Enter into long-term price guarantee forward contracts with trading partners, setting appropriate targets for price, volume, and duration for risk management in line with the Company's business plan.
  • Manage inventory appropriately and plan purchases in line with the sales plan to reduce the impact of price volatility on the Company's returns and operational results.

Supply Chain Disruption Risk

Nature of Risk:

The Company assesses that in 2025, volatility from Climate Change and geological disasters such as earthquakes in Thailand are significant factors that could lead to severe Supply Chain Disruption risks, which could have cascading effects on business processes.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Inventory management: Improve policies for safety stock of goods and raw materials to effectively manage transportation.
  • Develop contingency plans for crises covering the entire supply chain and conduct response drills.
  • Install work programs on mobile devices to support external operations.

Emerging Risks

Climate Change Risk

Nature of Risk:

Currently, natural disasters occur more frequently and with greater severity, mainly caused by climate change due to human activities such as greenhouse gas emissions and natural resource destruction. This issue has become a key expectation that customers and stakeholders have of the Company.

Risk Mitigation Measures:

The Company is committed to managing its business with strict consideration of ESG dimensions, both in preventing risks of supply chain disruption from natural disasters in 2025 and in elevating environmentally friendly operational standards.

  • Establish a working group and sustainability policies to evaluate, set targets, and operate in accordance with the Company's strategy.
  • Prepare the Corporate Carbon Footprint (CFO) report, which must be certified by the Thailand Greenhouse Gas Management Organization (Public Organization) and ISO 14064-1.
  • Define guidelines for managing Environmental, Social, and Governance aspects, involving participation from all departments to cultivate a corporate culture.
  • Develop project plans covering all 3 dimensions: Environmental, Social, and Governance.
