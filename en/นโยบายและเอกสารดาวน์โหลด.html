Risk Management Policy

The Company recognizes the importance of risk management for sustainable growth amidst changing business environments. Therefore, a risk management policy has been established according to international standards (COSO-ERM):

  • Participation and Awareness: Directors, executives, and all employees are responsible as Risk Owners for assessing, monitoring, and communicating risk management.
  • Integration with Strategy: Risk management is integrated into the Company's decision-making, strategy formulation, and operations.
  • Acceptable Level Management: Establish measures to reduce the likelihood and impact of risks at both corporate and departmental levels.
  • Continuous Assessment: Systematically monitor risks affecting goals and regularly review and improve processes to adapt to changes.
  • Regular Reporting: Report risk management, changes in risks, impacts, and mitigation guidelines to the Board and management regularly.

Main Roles and Responsibilities

  • Risk Management Committee: Consists of 4 directors. Oversees, advises, and reviews risk policies and mitigation measures to keep risks within the acceptable Risk Appetite, and reports to the Board.
  • Risk Management Working Group: Consists of heads of all 7 departments. Analyzes, monitors, and evaluates operational risks, develops management plans, and reports to the Risk Management Committee.

Strategic Risk

Risk from Few Suppliers and Changes in Supplier Policies

Nature of Risk:

The Company is a distributor of chemical products for leading domestic and international companies, specifically solvent chemicals. In 2025, there have been changes in both manufacturers (refineries) and supplier policies, such as adding distributors in every country, which may increase the Company's business competitors and result in a loss of market share.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Market Expansion: Allocating proactive budgets to reach new target groups and increase market share.
  • Building Good Customer Relationships: By offering products according to customer needs.
  • Internal Collaboration Integration: Coordinating between sales, services, and logistics to improve product delivery capabilities.
  • Proactive Satisfaction Management: Emphasizing immediate resolution of complaints, along with a satisfaction evaluation system for continuous improvement.

Capital Projects Risk

Nature of Risk:

The Company continuously invests to expand future growth and gain competitive advantages. Occasionally, this exposes the Company to the risk that returns on investment may not meet targets, or project execution may face delays.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Preparing investment plans, which requires risk analysis and return on investment evaluation, presenting the investment plans to the Risk Management Committee for consideration and opinion, and submitting them to the Board of Directors' meeting.
  • Assigning project managers responsible for monitoring, evaluating, resolving issues, and reporting to the Executive Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Board of Directors.
  • Continuously evaluating investment performance to enable timely assessment and problem resolution.
  • Establishing an investment working group to review the investment plans of the Company and its subsidiaries.

Dependency on Major Customers

Nature of Risk:

The Company faces challenges from relying on major customers amid macroeconomic risk factors, including the impacts of war and tariff policies that directly affect the purchasing power of trading partners. Furthermore, more new competitors entering the chemical industry have led to fierce price competition. The Company is therefore at risk if major customers reduce their order volumes or switch to competitors.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Market expansion: Allocating proactive budgets to reach new target groups and increase market share.
  • Building good customer relationships: By offering products according to customer needs.
  • Internal collaboration integration: Coordinating between sales, services, and logistics to improve product delivery capabilities.
  • Proactive satisfaction management: Emphasizing immediate resolution of complaints, along with a satisfaction evaluation system for continuous improvement.

Financial Risk

Interest Rate Fluctuation Risk

Nature of Risk:

Due to continuous business expansion and investment in strategic projects, the Company still faces challenges from stagnant interest rates and volatility in the global financial market, in order to strengthen its financial position in 2025.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Closely monitor news and policies of financial institutions. The finance department coordinates with financial institutions to assess and change the interest rate format at the appropriate time to lock in financial costs and reduce risks during periods of rising interest rates.
  • Manage the balance between cash inflows and outflows, including adjusting credit terms (given and received) to be consistent, and focus on optimal working capital turnover to reduce the need for additional borrowing and mitigate the impact of interest rate adjustments.
  • Enter into short-term loan interest rate contracts. In the case of rising interest rates, consider entering into short-term loan contracts by locking in longer credit terms to maintain the original interest rate for as long as possible.

Exchange Rate Fluctuation Risk

Nature of Risk:

Since the Company's business operations are linked to foreign markets, it inevitably faces risks from exchange rate fluctuations, especially in a highly uncertain global economic environment driven by the monetary policies of major central banks.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Assign personnel to closely monitor news and exchange rate movements on a daily basis to accurately assess currency trends, and coordinate and consult with partner banks to obtain their perspectives and currency trend analyses for decision-making.
  • Seek new financial instruments by studying and analyzing a wider variety of financial products to increase options for more flexible risk management.

Supply Chain Risk

Product Price Volatility Risk (High Costs)

Nature of Risk:

In 2025, the Thai economy is expected to expand, driven mainly by robust domestic consumption and public investment. However, the business sector still faces challenges from external factors such as trade wars, monetary policy directions of major powers, and volatility in global energy prices.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Monitor, analyze, and evaluate situations of factors affecting product price volatility, such as global oil prices, economic conditions, changes in supply and demand, and global climate change, to plan product trading.
  • Enter into long-term price guarantee forward contracts with trading partners, setting appropriate targets for price, volume, and duration for risk management in line with the Company's business plan.
  • Manage inventory appropriately and plan purchases in line with the sales plan to reduce the impact of price volatility on the Company's returns and operational results.

Supply Chain Disruption Risk

Nature of Risk:

The Company assesses that in 2025, volatility from Climate Change and geological disasters such as earthquakes in Thailand are significant factors that could lead to severe Supply Chain Disruption risks, which could have cascading effects on business processes.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Inventory management: Improve policies for safety stock of goods and raw materials to effectively manage transportation.
  • Develop contingency plans for crises covering the entire supply chain and conduct response drills.
  • Install work programs on mobile devices to support external operations.

Emerging Risks

Climate Change Risk

Nature of Risk:

Currently, natural disasters occur more frequently and with greater severity, mainly caused by climate change due to human activities such as greenhouse gas emissions and natural resource destruction. This issue has become a key expectation that customers and stakeholders have of the Company.

Risk Mitigation Measures:

The Company is committed to managing its business with strict consideration of ESG dimensions, both in preventing risks of supply chain disruption from natural disasters in 2025 and in elevating environmentally friendly operational standards.

  • Establish a working group and sustainability policies to evaluate, set targets, and operate in accordance with the Company's strategy.
  • Prepare the Corporate Carbon Footprint (CFO) report, which must be certified by the Thailand Greenhouse Gas Management Organization (Public Organization) and ISO 14064-1.
  • Define guidelines for managing Environmental, Social, and Governance aspects, involving participation from all departments to cultivate a corporate culture.
  • Develop project plans covering all 3 dimensions: Environmental, Social, and Governance.

IT & Operational Risk

Cybersecurity & IT Risk

Nature of Risk:

In the digital age, reliance on information systems for business operations has increased, exposing the Company to risks from cyberattacks, data breaches, or network outages, which could impact operational continuity and the Company's credibility.

Risk Mitigation Measures:
  • Strictly enforce the IT Security Policy and Operational Control Policy.
  • Develop an Incident Response and Prevention Plan to handle unexpected events.
  • Install network security systems, regularly back up data, and restrict access to critical information.

Internal Control

Union Petrochemical Public Company Limited or the "Company" has operated its internal controls according to international standards COSO (Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission) to enable the Company to achieve its three objectives: Operation, Reporting, and Compliance with laws and regulations related to the Company's business operations.

The Company's management and Internal Audit department have prepared the annual internal control assessment form according to the guidelines set by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). The assessment of the adequacy of the Company's internal control system has been reviewed, commented on, and recommended by the Audit Committee, and reported to the Board of Directors for consideration. In meeting No. 1/2025 on February 26, 2025, the Board of Directors opined that the Company's internal control system is adequate and appropriate, and no significant deficiencies were found that might affect the Company's internal control system. The report on the assessment of the adequacy of the internal control system includes clear, complete, accurate, and reliable reference documents for the assessment responses, and the business operations comply with relevant laws and regulations.

The Company has a department responsible for internal control and requires the internal control system to be integrated into all activities, departments, and levels within the Company to ensure an adequate and appropriate internal control system for business operations. In 2025, the Company has taken actions to increase the efficiency of the internal control system, with the performance summarized across the 5 components as follows:

1) Control Environment

The Board of Directors and management focus on ensuring personnel have the knowledge and capabilities to support sustainable corporate business growth. Thus, a Business Code of Conduct and corporate governance have been established to provide a good and appropriate internal control system as follows:

  • Policies and Code of Conduct: Establishing Good Corporate Governance Policy, Anti-Corruption Policy, Business Code of Conduct requirements, regulations, disciplinary rules, and monitoring compliance annually, along with communicating them to directors, executives, and employees for adherence.
  • Board Independence: The Board of Directors is independent of the management, with clear scopes of authority and duties. It oversees the overall operations, provides opinions on strategic direction, and regularly monitors performance.
  • Organizational Structure: Defining the organizational structure and lines of command for efficient management. This includes segregating duties in key functions, defining approval authority for checks and balances, and establishing a personnel development plan, succession plan, and systematic performance evaluation.

2) Risk Assessment

The Board of Directors and management prioritize risk management to achieve set targets and guide the Company towards sustainable growth by establishing a 4-member Risk Management Committee to oversee appropriate risk management aligned with strategy.

  • Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Framework: Analyzing, assessing, monitoring, and formulating risk management plans at the Corporate Risk, Operation Risk, and Projects Risk levels, including consideration for corruption risks.
  • Monitoring and Reporting: Risk owners define KPIs and report a monthly summary to the Executive Committee, and a corporate-level report to the Board of Directors quarterly, to assess residual risks to an acceptable level.
  • Crisis Response Plan: Developing risk management systems to accommodate and manage emergencies, enabling Business Continuity Planning (BCP) to reduce impacts and maintain corporate image.

3) Control Activities

The Company has established effective control activities to help mitigate risks to an acceptable level, including written procedures, policies, manuals, and operational steps.

  • Defining Scope of Authority: Decentralizing approval authority, segregating duties, implementing policies regarding Conflict of Interest transactions, and conducting business transparently, accountably, and fairly.
  • ISO 9001:2015 Standard: Operating according to standards, reviewing operations for compliance with rules and regulations to ensure efficiency and adequate internal control.

4) Information & Communication

The Company places importance on information systems and the use of information both internally and externally, promoting modern IT development.

  • Data Security: Defining access rights to key databases, auditing rights annually, and establishing policies for Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, and handling Market Sensitive Information.
  • Technology Utilization: Using the Wisible system for the sales process to track and monitor anomalies, and the ERP system to control credit and reduce bad debt risks, resulting in achieving debt collection targets.
  • IT Security & PDPA Policy: Establishing IT security policies, data backup, and emergency response, along with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) policy, comprehensively communicated to employees, and providing transparent complaint channels.
  • IT System Audit: Controlling approval limits in the IT system, with operational audits conducted by IT Audit for continuous internal control improvement.

5) Monitoring Activities

Internal Audit System: The Internal Audit department is independent and reports directly to the Audit Committee, providing assurance and advisory services to ensure corporate governance, risk management, and internal controls operate effectively.

  • Annual Audit Plan: Formulating an annual internal audit plan to assess internal control systems of various processes.
  • Reporting and Tracking: Reporting audit results to the Audit Committee quarterly, and continuously monitoring the implementation of corrective actions based on recommendations.
  • Independent Expression of Opinion: In performing their duties, internal auditors face no restrictions in expressing opinions and have no conflicts of interest with the audited departments.
