Union Petrochemical Public Company Limited

Business Policy and Overview

Union Petrochemical Public Company Limited is one of the leaders in importing and distributing chemicals and solvents. The company has a long history of operation, stepping into its 45th year with the ability to adapt its general standard solvents and specialty chemicals to suit specific industries. The company adheres to a business approach that focuses on sustainable growth, a comprehensive variety of products to meet the needs of customers in various industries, standardized warehouse and logistics management, and fast transportation. We emphasize quality and standards in sourcing from reliable product origins, as well as building strong business relationships with both suppliers and customers.

Vision

To be a leader in providing comprehensive chemical distribution services with a variety of products and chemical services covering the supply chain of customers in all industries.

"Integration Beyond A Chemical Distributor with Diverse Services for Sustainability"

Mission

  1. Build a diverse chemical business network to cover the supply chain in businesses that produce and use chemicals.
  2. Develop new businesses and services to create comprehensive chemical service solutions that can quickly respond to market demands.
  3. Promote the use of innovation and technology to increase the efficiency of delivering value in the company's products and services.
  4. Develop and enhance the potential of personnel in both the form of upgrading work skills (Reskill) and creating new work skills that respond to new business changes (Upskill) to achieve work efficiency and better quality of life.
  5. Create awareness of maximizing resource utilization to cover activities in the company's business chain and drive practices at the employee and management levels to build an environmentally friendly corporate culture.
  6. Manage the organization according to good corporate governance principles and comply with laws related to business operations in the economic, social, and environmental dimensions to build confidence among stakeholders and for sustainable growth.

4 Main Business Groups

Currently, the company operates in 4 main business groups:

Group 1: General Chemicals and Solvents
(Commodity Chemical and Solvent)

Divided into 2 main parts: Solvents and other chemical products. For solvents, it can be divided into 7 categories:

Alcohol Paint, chemical production, packaging, ink industries
Aliphatic hydrocarbon Paint and other industries
Aromatic hydrocarbon Paint, chemical production, packaging industries
Ester Paint, packaging and other industries
Glycols Chemical production industry
Ketone Paint, chemical production, and packaging industries
Monomer Main component in other industries

Group 2: Specialty Chemicals and Solvents
(Specialty Chemical and Solvent)

Distributed through affiliated company Lion Asia (Thailand) Co., Ltd. focusing on creating value through Custom Formulation.

Key Features:

• Adjust properties to suit specific production processes
• Increase dissolving efficiency, reduce overall raw material usage
• Environmentally friendly and high safety in usage
• Reduce odor, color, or hazardous substances as specified

Group 3: Rubber and Polymer
(Rubber and Polymer)

The company distributes plastic pellets and raw materials necessary for rubber production to the packaging industry and the medical device manufacturing industry.

Group 4: Other Support and Innovation
(Support, Innovation and Technology)

Responding to global macro changes (Global Mega Trends) and economic promotion policies both domestically and internationally. Business and investment feasibility analysis includes:

• Manufacturing and distributing all types of products from coconut processing
• Short-term lending for car dealership business (Floor Plan)
• Consulting, training, corporate strategy development, and sourcing environmental innovations
• Business related to recycling used materials

Key Milestones & Developments

Significant steps driving UKEM's success and sustainable growth

2025

  • May 23, 2025: Verified and certified greenhouse gas emissions report according to ISO 14064-1 standard by MASCI
  • Jun 24, 2025: Received Carbon Footprint of Organization (CFO) certification from Thailand Greenhouse Gas Management Organization (TGO). This reflects our commitment to standardizing the measurement and management of greenhouse gas emissions at an international level.

2024

  • Jun 5, 2024: The share repurchase program ended. The company repurchased 75.14 million ordinary shares totaling 63.50 million baht, with an accumulated total of 78.15 million shares, representing 6.73% of the total issued and paid-up shares. The total cost value was 65.92 million baht, and retained earnings were allocated as a reserve for treasury shares amounting to 65.92 million baht, shown in the statement of financial position under "Appropriated retained earnings - reserve for treasury shares".

2022

2021

  • Dec 20, 2021: Registered a decrease in paid-up capital for unsold repurchased shares
