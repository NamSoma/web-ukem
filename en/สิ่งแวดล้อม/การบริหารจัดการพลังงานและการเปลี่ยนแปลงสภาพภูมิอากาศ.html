Energy Management and Climate Change

Energy Management

Focusing on Energy Efficiency in an industry with high energy demand for electricity, heat, and fuel:

  • Reducing Fossil Fuel Reliance: Adopting modern technology and upgrading utility systems for maximum efficiency.
  • Monitoring and Tracking: Continuously monitoring energy consumption across all operational processes.
  • Corporate Culture: Fostering a corporate culture focused on energy conservation for long-term sustainable development.

Energy Management Approaches

Management Approach Participatory Practices
Energy Assessment and Monitoring
  • Establish real-time energy data collection systems for main processes and utility systems.
  • Analyze energy consumption trends to identify opportunities for efficiency improvement.
  • Establish Energy Baseline and Energy Performance Indicators (EnPIs) to compare operational results.
Energy Management System (EnMS) Implementation
  • Establish an energy working group and clearly define roles and responsibilities.
  • Develop short-term and long-term energy plans and targets.
  • Regularly review energy performance at the executive level.
Improving Energy Efficiency in Production Processes
  • Upgrade or replace machinery and equipment with high-efficiency or energy-saving alternatives.
  • Reduce energy loss from insulation, heat, and leakages.
Promoting Renewable Energy Usage
  • Study the feasibility of installing Solar PV systems within warehouses.
Management of Supporting Equipment and Utilities
  • Switch to LED lighting and implement automatic control systems.
  • Set appropriate air conditioning temperatures and utilize VRV or inverter systems.
  • Conduct Preventive Maintenance to reduce losses from defective equipment.
Building an Energy Conservation Culture
  • Organize training and internal communication to build energy awareness.
  • Encourage employee participation in proposing energy-saving ideas.
  • Organize campaigns or contests for energy reduction projects within departments.
Evaluation and Reporting of Energy Consumption
  • Prepare annual energy consumption and energy efficiency reports.
  • Continuously track the achievement of implemented measures by setting clear indicators and targets.
  • Use energy consumption data as a factor in business decision-making and environmental investments.

Energy Management: Fuel Consumption

For the management of energy derived from fossil fuels, the Company has formulated plans and undertaken the construction of a new warehouse in Chonburi Province. In addition to enhancing storage efficiency, this reduces the rate of fossil fuel consumption for receiving goods from Laem Chabang Port and delivering them to customers operating in the Eastern Economic Corridor, as the construction site is located in the Rojana Laem Chabang Industrial Estate.

Based on the Company's fuel consumption data for 2024-2025, the consumption of diesel oil—the primary fuel for transportation and logistics operations—decreased significantly from 290,456.06 liters in 2024 to 188,991.57 liters in 2025. Meanwhile, the use of gasohol trended slightly downwards, reflecting efficiency in energy management, optimal transportation planning, and cost-effective resource utilization.

Greenhouse Gas Emissions Management

Systematic Greenhouse Gas Management: To mitigate environmental impact, respond to SDGs, and build confidence aligned with our ESG strategy.

  • International Standards: Operating under the ISO 14064-1 framework and guidelines from the Thailand Greenhouse Gas Management Organization.
  • Clean Technology: Applying clean technology and enhancing energy efficiency in production processes.
  • Supply Chain Management: Developing decarbonization approaches collaboratively with suppliers for long-term sustainability.

Corporate Greenhouse Gas Emission Reduction Guidelines

Scope 1: Direct Emissions from Organization-Controlled Sources
  • Control and reduce gas leakages from cooling systems.
  • Promote the use of Electric Vehicles (EVs) or biodiesel in internal plant activities.
  • Modify production processes to consume less energy or generate less waste.
Scope 2: Indirect Emissions from Purchased Energy
  • Switch to renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, or biomass power.
  • Install Solar Rooftop systems on buildings or plants.
  • Improve energy efficiency in buildings and utility systems (e.g., LED, inverters, BMS).
  • Conduct Energy Audits and implement recommendations to enhance energy performance.
  • Explore the feasibility of Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) or Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs).
Scope 3: Other Indirect Emissions Across the Value Chain Reducing emissions from upstream activities:
  • Source raw materials with low carbon efficiency or environmental certifications.
  • Collaborate with suppliers to improve energy efficiency and reduce carbon emissions.
  • Minimize transportation distances and frequencies by optimizing logistics systems.
  • Encourage the use of recycled or reusable raw materials.

Reducing emissions from downstream activities:
  • Develop post-use packaging or product management guidelines (e.g., reuse, recycle).
  • Utilize energy-efficient product transportation modes such as rail, ship, or clean energy vehicles.
  • Promote a low-carbon corporate culture, such as hosting online meetings instead of traveling.
