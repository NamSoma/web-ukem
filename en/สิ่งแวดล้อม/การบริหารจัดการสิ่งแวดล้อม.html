Environmental Policy and Practices

The Company emphasizes conducting business with social and environmental responsibility, striving for sustainable business growth coupled with maintaining ecosystem balance. The Company has established environmental policies and practices as a framework for operations, focusing on maximizing resource efficiency in all operational processes. This aims to minimize environmental impacts around the Company's warehouse areas and throughout its value chain as follows:
  • The Company prioritizes caring for, protecting, and maintaining the environment around employees' workplaces in compliance with the law, as well as adhering to standards, criteria, and certifications at both national and international levels.
  • To ensure efficient and optimal resource utilization for business operations, the Company has set objectives, targets, and operational plans focusing on cost-effective resource use, along with managing water and air pollution within legally prescribed limits.
  • The Company emphasizes waste management according to the 3Rs principles: Reduce unnecessary resource consumption, Reuse to minimize waste generation, and Recycle resources. This is to maximize efficiency in reducing waste volume and environmental impact.
  • The Company places great importance on strict compliance with laws related to energy management, the environment, and climate, as well as improving energy efficiency suitable for operational characteristics, production technologies, and internationally recognized best practices.
  • Executives and employees at all levels recognize the importance of managing energy, the environment, and climate, and must cooperate in implementing established measures. Communication is also maintained to build understanding and ensure proper execution.
  • The Company provides necessary resources—personnel, budget, time, and training—to ensure efficient energy, environmental, and climate management. Furthermore, it encourages employees to participate in proposing ideas beneficial to the organization's environmental development.
  • To raise awareness of biodiversity, the Company encourages employees and stakeholders to understand the impacts of business operations and instills a sense of responsibility regarding activities affecting biodiversity.
  • The Company is committed to fostering a corporate culture that values the environment, promoting a sense of environmental stewardship among employees and stakeholders. It also drives subsidiaries under its control to strictly adhere to environmental policies.
  • The Company aims to promote the communication, dissemination, and public relations of environmental policies and practices to employees and stakeholders through corporate activities. Additionally, it prepares annual performance summary reports and discloses information to stakeholders to ensure transparency and continuous monitoring of environmental performance.

Water Management

The petrochemical and chemical distribution business necessitates water usage in various operational areas, such as cleaning tanks and equipment, quality control, warehouse management, and maintaining environmental and safety conditions within operational sites. Efficient water use and appropriate water management are essential components in reducing natural resource impacts, alongside tackling challenges posed by climate change and water resource security.

Furthermore, the Company recognizes its role and responsibility in sustainable water resource management. It has established measures to appropriately control and enhance water efficiency in its operations. These include regular inspection and monitoring of water usage, process improvements to reduce unnecessary consumption, and managing wastewater treatment systems in compliance with relevant laws and standards. This supports responsible environmental business operations and creates sustainable value for all stakeholder groups.

Waste Management

The petrochemical and chemical distribution business has the potential to generate both hazardous and non-hazardous waste across multiple operational stages, such as chemical storage and transfer, tank and equipment cleaning, wastewater treatment, pollution control, as well as office and warehouse support activities. Consequently, systematic and responsible waste management is a crucial issue reflecting the organization's commitment to sustainable business operations, considering the impacts on the environment, communities, and surrounding ecosystems.

The Company emphasizes waste management at every step, from waste segregation and classification, proper and safe storage, transportation, to disposal or utilization by legally authorized service providers. The Company focuses on source reduction (Reduce), reuse (Reuse), and recycling (Recycle), along with selecting appropriate waste management methods to minimize the volume of waste destined for disposal and effectively reduce environmental impacts. The Company operates in strict compliance with relevant laws, environmental standards, and regulations to build confidence among all stakeholder groups.

Management Guidelines

1) Academic and Legal Waste Management

The Company prioritizes waste management to strictly and continuously reduce environmental impacts as mandated by law. It adheres to the 3Rs waste management principles, which align with both domestic and international practices: Reduce (reducing usage), Reuse (reusing waste), and Recycle (bringing back for new use). Most of the waste generated comes from office and hazardous warehouse areas and is classified as general waste, which must comply with relevant rules, laws, and regulations, notably the Public Health Act B.E. 2535. The Company does not produce waste from production processes that falls under industrial waste. General waste generated within the Company is divided into 4 groups: organic waste, recyclable waste, general waste, and hazardous waste. Current waste management focuses on reducing waste generation at the source, sorting and collecting, and sending it for proper disposal.

2) Waste Management Through Employee Participation

Sustainable waste management in the industrial sector cannot be achieved solely through technology or environmental measures; it requires the cooperation of personnel at all organizational levels. They play a vital role in promoting behaviors that reduce, separate, and properly manage waste on an ongoing basis.

The Company recognizes the importance of employee participation in waste management. It has developed guidelines and activities that encourage personnel to engage in every management step, from source segregation and reducing the use of waste-generating materials, to forwarding waste into proper recycling or disposal processes. This aims to elevate the efficiency of the waste management system and build an environmentally responsible corporate culture.

Such operations not only help reduce environmental impacts but also foster a sense of sustainability among employees, increase transparency in waste management, and tangibly support the organization's ESG goals and circular economy. Management Guidelines Participation Principles
Education and Training on Waste Management
  • Organize training or knowledge-sharing activities on waste types, proper waste segregation, and the environmental impact of waste.
  • Create PR materials such as vinyl banners, posters, or video clips that are easy to understand and accessible for employees.
Installing Waste Separation Systems in the Workplace
  • Clearly place segregated waste bins in work areas, complete with labels and usage guidelines.
  • Set up drop-off points for hazardous waste from employees, such as batteries, light bulbs, or chemical packaging.
Creating Activities to Promote Participation
  • Organize "Proper Waste Segregation" or "Departmental Waste Reduction" contests with rewards or certificates of recognition.
  • Promote corporate recycling activities, such as accepting recyclable waste donations in exchange for prizes, or partnering with external agencies to repurpose waste.
  • Activities to exchange waste for useful items (Waste to Gift / Waste Exchange Day).
Integrating Waste Data into the Corporate Information System
  • Involve employees in reporting waste volumes from their departments to analyze and plan waste reduction.
  • Use digital systems or online forms for employees to report waste reduction methods or actionable ideas.
Integrating "Zero Waste" Concepts into Daily Behavior
  • Encourage employees to use personal containers and abstain from single-use plastics.
  • Campaign for 3Rs behaviors: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle through internal activities.
  • Establish an environmental volunteer network among employees (Green Volunteer / Waste Watcher).
Communication and Performance Reporting
  • Communicate successful waste reduction results or managed waste volumes through internal channels like Intranet, E-mail, and bulletin boards.
  • Link employee participation to ESG targets and feature them in the sustainability report.
