Sustainability Policy in the Social Dimension

Committed to developing business alongside social responsibility, strictly adhering to laws and international practices to become an ASEAN leader as an Integrated Chemicals Provider and Solution Service Platform:

  • Business Operations: Emphasizes transparency, good corporate governance, and building trust among stakeholders.
  • Human Rights: Respects human dignity, avoids discrimination, and prevents rights violations (e.g., child or forced labor).
  • Social Care: Provides complaint channels for affected parties and supports underprivileged or disabled groups.

Respects human dignity and provides equality to all stakeholder groups:

  • Non-discrimination: Treats everyone equally regardless of race, religion, gender, age, or physical condition.
  • Prevention & Monitoring: Prevents human rights violations such as child labor, forced labor, and sexual harassment.
  • Listening Channels: Establishes grievance mechanisms and remedies for those affected by business operations.

Manages business with transparency and ethics:

  • Zero Bribery: Strictly prohibits giving or receiving bribes to government or private sector officials.
  • Internal Controls: Clearly structures the organization to ensure proper checks and balances.
  • Certification: Certified member of the Thai Private Sector Collective Action Against Corruption (latest certification: June 30, 2025).

The Company recognizes the importance of human resource development and fair labor practices, which are factors that help increase corporate value and strengthen the Company's future competitiveness and sustainable growth. Therefore, the Company has established the following policies and practices:

  • Labor Rights: Respect employees' rights according to labor laws and human rights principles, providing fair employment processes and conditions.
  • Personnel Development: Promote development through training and seminars to enhance knowledge, capabilities, good attitudes, and teamwork.
  • Welfare Benefits: Provide legal and additional benefits such as health insurance, accident insurance, scholarships, and various financial assistance.
  • Health & Safety: Offer annual health check-ups and maintain a safe, sanitary workplace with strict accident prevention measures.
  • Grievance Channels: Provide opportunities to express opinions or complain about unfair treatment, ensuring protection for whistleblowers.

The Company is committed to developing its products and services to build trust and customer satisfaction, adhering to responsible and honest treatment of customers under the following policies:

  • Quality & Standards: Deliver products and services that meet customer-specified standards, continuously improving service delivery for optimal quality.
  • Safety: Ensure customer products are safely stored and delivered in compliance with national and international safety regulations.
  • Fair Marketing: Provide accurate information without distortion or exaggerated advertising, allowing customers to make informed decisions.
  • Customer Relations: Maintain an effective communication and complaint resolution system to rapidly respond to customer needs.
  • Confidentiality: Strictly safeguard customer information and prevent it from being used for improper purposes.

  • Partners: Adhere to fair contractual practices, comply with anti-corruption measures, respect property rights, avoid exploitation, and encourage joint CSR activities.
  • Creditors: Strictly comply with all agreements. If unable to repay debts as scheduled, notify creditors in advance to jointly find solutions.
  • Competitors: Engage in fair business competition without using bullying, exclusion, or illegal actions that result in unfair market conditions.

Strengthens the community and society by cooperating and assisting through:

  • Local Employment: Promoting opportunities for local labor to work within the organization.
  • Impact Mitigation: Swiftly and efficiently responding to incidents affecting the community from operations.
  • Awareness Building: Encouraging employees to participate in environmental and social responsibility with external networks.

Focuses on developing innovation in work processes to build sustainability:

  • 3Rs Innovation: Encouraging employees to invent methods for efficient resource use, waste management, and emission control.
  • Knowledge Sharing: Disclosing socially and environmentally beneficial innovations to stimulate further development.

Systematically evaluates and monitors progress:

  • Continuous Monitoring: Regularly reviewing and evaluating current and future social responsibility projects.
  • Data Disclosure: Reporting sustainability performance under GRI Standards in the 56-1 One Report (since 2021).
