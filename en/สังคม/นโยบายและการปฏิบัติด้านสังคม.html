Sustainability Policy in the Social Dimension

Union Petrochemical Public Company Limited is committed to developing its business alongside social responsibility, strictly adhering to laws, relevant regulations, and international practices. This creates a foundation for responsible, transparent, and sustainable long-term business operations. The Company has established a corporate social responsibility policy to serve as a systematic operational framework.

The Company aims to become an ASEAN leader in chemical distribution and as an Integrated Chemicals Provider and Solution Service Platform across the value chain, from upstream to downstream, for chemical-utilizing industries. We focus on building trust among investors, customers, and stakeholders through business operations grounded in good corporate governance, adding value to the organization, and promoting stable and sustainable growth.

The Company has a policy to support and respect the protection of human rights. This involves treating all involved parties—employees, communities, and society at large—with respect for human dignity, considering equal rights and liberties, avoiding violation of basic rights, and refraining from discrimination based on race, nationality, religion, language, skin color, gender, age, education, physical condition, or social status. This includes ensuring that the Company is not complicit in human rights abuses such as child labor, forced labor, and sexual harassment. Furthermore, the Company encourages the monitoring of human rights compliance by providing opportunities for participation, voicing opinions, and offering a complaint channel for those who suffer damage from rights violations arising from the Company's operations, providing remedies to the affected parties. The Company also conducts training or disseminates knowledge on human rights and cultivates awareness among personnel to comply with human rights principles. Although the Company currently does not have employees who are disabled or from underprivileged groups, it has contributed funds to the Fund for the Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

The Company manages its business based on transparency, ethics, and compliance with policies and laws related to preventing and combating corruption, as well as the giving or receiving of bribes or undue benefits to government officials or the private sector. The Company structures its organization to clearly divide responsibilities, work processes, and chains of command within each department to ensure appropriate checks and balances. To this end, the Company has prepared an Anti-Corruption Measures Manual compiling the code of conduct, regulations, and operational procedures for directors, executives, and employees to follow. The Company has demonstrated a firm commitment to fighting corruption by joining the Thai Private Sector Collective Action Against Corruption (CAC) in 2018, receiving its latest certification on June 30, 2025. (Anti-Corruption Policy: Company Website https://www.unionpetrochemical.com/)
The Company recognizes the importance of human resource development and fair labor practices, which are factors that help increase corporate value and strengthen the Company's future competitiveness and sustainable growth. Therefore, the Company has established the following policies and practices:
Respect employees' rights according to labor laws and human rights principles.
Provide fair employment processes, working conditions, and compensation determination.
Promote personnel development by organizing training, seminars, and workshops, as well as sending personnel to attend relevant academic seminars and training to develop their knowledge, capabilities, and potential. This includes cultivating good attitudes, ethics, morals, and teamwork.
Provide various welfare benefits for employees as required by law, such as Social Security and Provident Funds, as well as those beyond legal requirements, such as health insurance, accident insurance, and life insurance. This also includes providing various types of financial assistance to employees, such as scholarships for children, travel allowances, and cremation assistance funds.
Provide annual health check-up services for personnel at all levels of the Company, considering risk factors based on rank, age, gender, and individual working environments.
Ensure employees can work safely and hygienically in a good workplace by implementing accident prevention measures, fostering safety awareness, organizing training, promoting good employee hygiene, and maintaining a consistently sanitary and safe workplace.
Provide opportunities for employees to express opinions or complain about unfair treatment or improper actions within the Company, including protecting employees who report such issues.
The Company is committed to developing its products and services to build trust and customer satisfaction, adhering to responsible and honest treatment of customers under the following policies:
The Company prioritizes product quality and standards, focusing on preparing products and services that meet customer-specified standards and consistently improving service delivery so customers receive the highest quality and most efficient products and services.
The Company considers safety in providing storage and delivery services to customers and is committed to ensuring customers receive quality products that are safe according to national and international safety standards and regulations.
The Company adheres to fair marketing by ensuring customers receive accurate information about the Company's products without distortion, ambiguity, or exaggerated advertising, empowering customers with correct and sufficient information to decide on using the Company's products and services.
The Company maintains a customer relations system for effective communication, including efficiently receiving complaints about product and service quality to respond to customer needs rapidly.
The Company strictly safeguards customer information confidentiality and does not use such information for improper purposes.
The Company adheres to fair contractual practices with partners, complies with anti-corruption measures, respects partners' intellectual property or ownership rights, does not exploit partners, and encourages them to engage in corporate social responsibility activities together with the Company.
The Company complies with agreements made with creditors in various areas. If the Company is unable to repay debts as scheduled, the Company will notify creditors to jointly find a solution.
The Company engages in fair business competition and does not use bullying, exclusion, or any actions prohibited by law to prevent unfair competition.
The Company realizes its responsibility to the community and society to strengthen them. Therefore, the Company has a policy to assist and develop society by cooperating in various activities with surrounding communities in operational areas as appropriate, promoting local labor employment opportunities, and responding to incidents affecting the community, society, and environment arising from the Company's operations swiftly and efficiently. Furthermore, the Company encourages employees to have environmental and social awareness and responsibility, and collaborates among groups or networks to develop targeted communities or societies each year.
The Company encourages employees to invent innovations that respond to various social responsibility policies, such as the efficient use of water and energy resources, 3Rs waste management, and waste emission control. The Company focuses on using innovations to improve or develop work processes within the Company to yield more efficient or effective results, and will disclose socially and environmentally beneficial innovations appropriately to stimulate further innovation development.
The Company recognizes the importance of continuous operations with a view toward social responsibility. The Company will monitor, evaluate, and review currently supported projects and will carefully, continuously, and consistently consider future social responsibility projects. The Company has reported its sustainable development results under the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards framework, disclosing economic, environmental, and social data, beginning in 2021, and such disclosures are included in the 56-1 One Report annual data filing.
