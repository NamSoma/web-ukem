Fair Labor Practices

The Company recognizes the importance of human resource development and fair labor practices as essential factors that help increase corporate value and strengthen the Company's competitiveness and sustainable future growth. Therefore, the Company has established the following policies and practices:

Focuses on fair recruitment and employment processes, adhering to human rights, international labor standards, and ESG principles:

  • Equality: Evaluates applicants equally without discrimination based on gender, age, race, or religion.
  • Fair Compensation: Establishes appropriate compensation and benefits aligned with industry standards.
  • Safe Environment: Respects diversity, provides opportunities for participation, and ensures a physically and mentally safe workplace.

Recruits personnel fairly, transparently, and auditably:

  • Merit-Based: Selects candidates based primarily on merit, ability, and suitability for the position.
  • Clear Procedures: Public job announcements, systematic screening, neutral interviews, and transparent results.
  • Anti-Illegal Labor: Does not support forced labor, child labor, or any human rights violations.

Evaluates performance fairly and transparently based on Good HR Practices to promote personnel growth and elevate organizational efficiency.

Employees are the most valuable resource: Employee Engagement is crucial for enhancing operational efficiency, stability, and sustainable growth.

Treats employees equally: No employee will be terminated without reasonable cause unless they commit a severe disciplinary offense or meet other conditions outlined in the Company's regulations, executed with transparency, fairness, and legality.

Secure Complaint Channels: Provides internal and external channels for complaints and whistleblowing with strict confidentiality and protection measures for complainants to ensure transparency and fairness.

Respect for Human Rights

The Company has a policy to be fair to all stakeholders by complying with relevant laws and regulations and adhering to basic human rights principles according to international standards without discrimination based on origin, race, gender, age, skin color, religion, disability, financial status, family background, educational institution, or any other status not directly related to work performance. The Company also maintains a working environment free from personal rights violations, with mutual respect, cooperation, assistance, offering suggestions, and solving problems together at work.

Fair Labor Practices

The Company recognizes that employees are a highly valuable resource of the company. Therefore, the Company aims to support the development of human resource potential for maximum benefit to the Company, as well as encourage employees to participate in creating a good work culture and atmosphere, promote teamwork, equally and equitably listen to opinions and suggestions from employees at all levels, and provide appropriate and fair compensation and welfare benefits.

The Company promotes employee knowledge, abilities, skills, and experience to lead to efficient performance by providing training for employees in various departments. In addition, the Company sends employees for external training to increase knowledge in various areas related to their work, and bring the knowledge gained from the training back to transfer to their colleagues, such as training on newly issued accounting standards, computer network systems training, etc.
