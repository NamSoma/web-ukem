Fair Labor Practices

The Company recognizes the importance of human resource development and fair labor practices as essential factors that help increase corporate value and strengthen the Company's competitiveness and sustainable future growth. Therefore, the Company has established the following policies and practices:

Union Petrochemical Public Company Limited or (“The Company”) recognizes its role and responsibility in conducting business with integrity, transparency, and sustainability. It therefore places great importance on fair recruitment and employment processes, adhering to basic human rights principles, international labor standards, and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) business practices. This is to create a safe, fair work environment free from discrimination, offering all employees the opportunity to fully develop their potential.

In the recruitment and employment process, the Company focuses on considering applicants equally, without segregation or discrimination based on gender, age, race, religion, beliefs, disability, or social status. It also establishes appropriate and fair compensation and welfare benefits aligned with industry standards to motivate and provide job security for employees at all levels. Furthermore, the Company promotes diversity and inclusion within the organization by respecting personnel differences, providing opportunities for employees to participate in expressing opinions and making decisions related to their work. It also supports continuous career development, the right to form groups appropriately according to corporate culture and context, and creates a physically and mentally safe work environment.

The Company firmly believes that operating on a foundation of fairness and respect for human rights will lead to creating shared value between the organization and its stakeholders, as well as supporting the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), a key guideline for driving the organization towards stable long-term growth.

The Company prioritizes fair, transparent, and auditable personnel recruitment and selection by conducting employment processes under the principles of respect for human rights, non-discrimination, and providing equal opportunities to all applicants without segregation based on gender, age, race, skin color, religion, social status, disability, political beliefs, sexual orientation, or any other status.

The Company adheres to employment principles that respect labor rights and workers' freedom, encouraging employees to express opinions, associate, or negotiate peacefully and appropriately within the bounds of law and corporate culture. It does not support forced labor, child labor, or any actions that violate human rights.

In the personnel recruitment and selection process, the Company primarily focuses on merit, ability, and suitability for the position through clear steps: announcing job openings via public channels, systematically screening applications, conducting interviews by a neutral committee, and transparently notifying results.

The Company is committed to promoting personnel diversity to foster a corporate culture of mutual respect, leading to innovation, participation, and sustainable mutual growth.

Therefore, the Company implements a systematic, transparent, and equal personnel recruitment and selection process to obtain qualified individuals suited for their positions, focusing primarily on capability and without discrimination at any stage.

The Company prioritizes fair, transparent, and systematic employee performance evaluation to promote personnel growth alongside elevating organizational efficiency, conducting continuous evaluations based on Good HR Practices.

The Company realizes that employees are the organization's most valuable resource. Employee Engagement is a crucial factor in enhancing operational efficiency, corporate stability, and sustainable growth.

The Company considers employees its most valuable resource and therefore focuses on building a good corporate culture and working atmosphere, treating employees equally. Accordingly, no employee will be terminated without reasonable cause unless the employee commits a severe disciplinary offense according to the Company's rules and regulations, or if there is any other cause stipulated by the Company's working regulations defining such cause. Thus, the Company has clear procedures in place, executed with transparency, fairness, and legality, without discrimination.

The Company is mindful of the proper performance of duties by all parties and has thus provided channels for receiving complaints and whistleblowing from both outside and inside the Company. It also maintains an appropriate complaint management system and has established measures to protect and maintain the confidentiality of complainants, encouraging the Company's personnel to conduct business correctly, transparently, fairly, and auditably.
