The Company gives the highest priority to the occupational health and safety of employees, partners, contractors, and all stakeholders. This is a fundamental basis for responsible and sustainable business operations, especially in high-risk industrial sectors such as those involving hazardous chemicals, large machinery, confined space operations, or environments that may pose hazards. The Company well realizes that preventing these risks is not only a matter of safety but is also directly related to the quality of life of personnel and the organization's image.

To manage potential risks, the Company has developed an occupational health and safety management system in line with the international standard ISO 45001 and the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) 403: Occupational Health and Safety reporting guidelines, focusing on three key principles:

  • Prevention – Focusing on reducing and controlling risk factors that may lead to accidents or occupational diseases.
  • Participation – Encouraging employees to play a role in every step of safety management, such as proposing preventive measures, acting as Safety Champions, or participating in safety inspection activities in operational areas.
  • Continuous Improvement – Regularly inspecting, reviewing, and developing the safety system to keep up with changes in the environment, technology, and legal requirements.

The Company has established clear safety goals, such as reducing the accident rate to zero, reducing the number of Lost Time Injuries (LTI), and improving the safety level in high-risk areas, with tangible support measures including:

  • Continuous safety training, both theoretical and practical.
  • Systematic Risk Assessment operations.
  • Promoting Near Miss Reporting.
  • Accident investigation and Root Cause Analysis to prevent recurrence.
  • Proactive communication and cultivating a "Safety is Everyone's Duty" culture as part of the organization's work processes.

Internal Communication

The Company has fostered operational participation at all levels by establishing the Occupational Safety, Health, and Working Environment Committee. This comprises representatives at the supervisory level and employee representatives to jointly survey unsafe working conditions, consider safety, occupational health, and environmental work plans and policies, monitor compliance with laws, and communicate to prevent and reduce work-related accidents or illnesses through various channels, such as:

  • Sending work safety information via email and Social Media.
  • Pre-work meetings for employees, such as Morning Talks.
Year 2025

Organizing Health Promotion Activities

The Company arranges health check-ups for new employees and annual health check-ups for all employees based on occupational risk factors, monitoring potential impacts from operations. This includes lung function tests, hearing tests, occupational vision tests, and heavy metal screening, managed under the employee health and illness database system. Additionally, health promotion activities are conducted in accordance with Happy Workplace principles.

Year 2025

Building Corporate Safety Awareness and Culture

Building safety awareness and culture is at the heart of conducting business in the chemical industry. The Company therefore emphasizes cultivating the concept that "Safety is Everyone's Duty" through continuous training, proactive preventive measures, and encouraging employee participation at all levels. It also adopts technology and international standards to enhance a safe working environment and reduce operational risks. In 2025, the Company organized various training and drill plans for Emergency Preparedness as follows:

  • Occupational Health, Safety, and Environment Training.
  • Basic Firefighting and Fire Evacuation Drills.
  • Chemical Spill Response Drills.
  • First Aid Training.
Year 2025

Occupational Health, Safety, and Working Environment Operations

The Company conducts its business with strict consideration for occupational safety and the environment, both within and around its operational sites. The Company has therefore integrated occupational health and safety management principles as part of its operational guidelines to ensure efficiency, establishing the following occupational safety, health, and working environment policy:

  • The Company will implement and develop an appropriate occupational health and safety management system in compliance with laws and other requirements set by the Company.
  • The Company will support the improvement of working conditions and environments to ensure physical safety and foster a healthy mental working environment to enhance operational efficiency.
  • The Company considers safety operations as the responsibility of executives and employees at all levels, emphasizing the safety of themselves, colleagues, and company property throughout their working hours.
  • The Company provides hygienic and adequate facilities, such as restrooms, standard drinking water dispensers (filters checked monthly), and standard warehouses.
  • The Company will continuously control and monitor the environment within and around the Company to ensure compliance with legally prescribed standards.
