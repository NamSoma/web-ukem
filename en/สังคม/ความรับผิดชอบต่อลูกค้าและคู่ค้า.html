The Company has established a clear 7-step complaint handling process, including:

  1. Receiving Complaints: Via Sales Department, LINE, Email by recording complete initial information.
  2. Problem Analysis: Verifying documentation, purchase orders, delivery notes, COA, or SPEC.
  3. Initial Customer Response: Within 1–3 business days with demonstrated care and attention.
  4. Corrective Action: Such as product replacement, on-site inspection, or offering discounts.
  5. Preventive Action: Analyzing the Root Cause and improving processes, such as through training or adding checklists.
  6. Case Closure and Follow-up: Informing the customer of the resolution and following up on satisfaction.
  7. Summary and Reporting: Reporting complaints monthly or quarterly, and presenting them to management for long-term improvement planning.

  • Creditors: The Company fulfills agreements with creditors. If unable to repay debts as scheduled, it will notify creditors to collaboratively find a resolution.
  • Competitors: The Company conducts business fairly, avoiding bullying, exclusion, or any illegal actions to prevent unfair competition.

The Company focuses on selecting capable partners who conduct business responsibly, using the following evaluation criteria:

  • 01. Reliability: Having a reliable operational and financial track record, including potential to grow alongside the Company in the long term.
  • 02. Quality: Manufacturing, distributing, or providing quality products/services that meet demands in quantity and price, with verifiable quality.
  • 03. Delivery: Evaluating punctuality in delivering goods and services.
  • 04. Coordination: Evaluating coordination capabilities, problem-solving skills, and team readiness.
  • 05. Human Rights Due Diligence: Planning Human Rights Due Diligence assessments for key partners' operations.
  • 06. ESG: Conducting business responsibly towards the Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG).

Establishing a Supplier Code of Conduct: In 2025, the Company is in the process of studying and formulating a Supplier Code of Conduct as a guideline for practices and evaluating partners. Progress will be reported next year.
