The Company has established a clear 7-step complaint handling process, including:

  1. Receiving Complaints: Via Sales Department, LINE, Email by recording complete initial information.
  2. Problem Analysis: Verifying documentation, purchase orders, delivery notes, COA, or SPEC.
  3. Initial Customer Response: Within 1–3 business days with demonstrated care and attention.
  4. Corrective Action: Such as product replacement, on-site inspection, or offering discounts.
  5. Preventive Action: Analyzing the Root Cause and improving processes, such as through training or adding checklists.
  6. Case Closure and Follow-up: Informing the customer of the resolution and following up on satisfaction.
  7. Summary and Reporting: Reporting complaints monthly or quarterly, and presenting them to management for long-term improvement planning.
Partners

The Company adheres to fair contractual practices with partners, complies with established anti-corruption measures, respects partners' intellectual property or ownership rights, does not exploit partners, and encourages partners to engage in joint social responsibility activities with the Company.

Creditors

The Company fulfills its agreements with creditors in various aspects. In the event that the Company is unable to repay debts as scheduled, the Company will notify creditors to collaboratively find a resolution.

Competitors

The Company conducts business competition fairly, avoiding bullying, exclusion, or any actions prohibited by law to prevent unfair competition.

The Company focuses on selecting capable partners who conduct business responsibly, using the following evaluation criteria:

01

Having a reliable operational and financial track record, including the potential to grow alongside the Company in the long term.

02

Manufacturing, distributing, or providing quality services that meet demands in both quantity and price, with verifiable quality.

03

Evaluating punctuality in delivering goods and services.

04

Evaluating coordination capabilities, problem-solving skills, and team readiness.

05

Planning Human Rights Due Diligence assessments for key partners' operations.

06

Conducting business responsibly towards the Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG).

Establishing a Supplier Code of Conduct: In 2025, the Company is in the process of studying and formulating a Supplier Code of Conduct to serve as a guideline for practices and for evaluating new and key partners. Progress on this will be reported in the following year.
